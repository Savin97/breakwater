## Base Rate

**NEEDS A REWRITE, NOT A NUMBER SWAP.** This post was built around a ~6x gap between the
base rate and the High Alert rate. The verified gap is 1.91x stratified, and the corrected
base rate (20.4%) undercuts the opening line that most earnings events do not produce large
moves. The corrected numbers are below and are correct; the argument around them is not,
and needs rebuilding around discrimination and capture rather than a dramatic ratio.

Corrected figures (audit §Q2, n=11,496 verified events):
- S&P 500 8%+ move rate across those events: about 20.4% [19.7, 21.1]
- Breakwater High Alert historical rate: about 45.5% [42.9, 48.2]
- Lift, stratified by announcement window: 1.91x (never quote the crude 2.23x)

The product is a risk radar, not a direction predictor.
