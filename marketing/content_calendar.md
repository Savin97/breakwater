# Breakwater Content Calendar

## Weekly Loop

Monday morning:
- Send the timely earnings risk digest.
- Publish a public X post that explains the weekly theme without giving away the full timely list.
- Update the landing page delayed track record through `recent_calls.json`.

Tuesday or Wednesday:
- Publish one ticker breakdown from a completed or already-public event.
- Explain why Breakwater flagged it or why it stayed normal.

Friday or following Monday:
- Publish last week's results chart.
- Include wins and misses. The point is a credible public record, not perfect hindsight.

Ongoing:
- Reply to earnings, options, and volatility discussions with useful context.
- Ask subscribers what they use Breakwater for: options, stock risk, portfolio monitoring, or research.

## X Cadence

Target 3-5 posts per week:
- 1 weekly risk-radar post.
- 1 results/track-record post.
- 1 educational model or base-rate post.
- 1 ticker breakdown.
- 0-1 founder note or dashboard progress post.

Every post should do one job:
- Prove the model has a real track record.
- Explain why earnings risk matters.
- Convert the right reader to the email digest or dashboard.

## Reddit Cadence

For the first 2-4 weeks, do not promote Breakwater directly from a low-karma account.

Comment on existing threads only:
- Earnings volatility questions.
- Implied move vs realized move discussions.
- Risk management around earnings.
- Historical post-earnings move analysis.

Only link Breakwater after the account has credible karma and the post is clearly framed as research or a delayed public record.

## Weekly Review

Every Friday, record:
- New email subscribers.
- X posts published.
- Best post by engagement.
- Dashboard clicks from public posts.
- Replies or conversations started.
- Subscriber use cases learned.
- One copy or product change for next week.
